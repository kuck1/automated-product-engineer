# Contributing

Contributions that make design handoffs more implementable, architecture guidance clearer, or verification more reliable are welcome.

1. Open an issue describing the problem and proposed outcome for material changes.
2. Keep examples framework-relevant, dependency-light, and free of private product information.
3. Prefer semantic, reusable guidance over organization-specific paths or tools.
4. Update the relevant prompt, template, checklist, and documentation together when their contract changes.
5. Verify Markdown links, JSON syntax, offline HTML behavior, and the absence of credentials or private URLs.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under the MIT License.
